Before you start

Good to know

How is a custom bracelet priced?

Pricing depends on the stones, metal, and details you choose. Vidhi sends a personalized quote before anything goes into production, so you know the price before you commit.

How long does a custom piece take to make?

Timelines vary by design and current order volume. Vidhi confirms an estimated timeline with you when your design is finalized, before your order is placed.

Can I choose gold or silver for any custom design?

Yes. Every custom line — birthstone, family, initial, and inspired-word — can be made in either gold or silver hardware.

What if I'm not sure which line is right for what I want?

That's what the first conversation is for. Tell Vidhi the idea or the person it's for, and she'll recommend which line, stones, and format fit best.
